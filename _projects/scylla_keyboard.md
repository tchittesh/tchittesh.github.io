`[LEFT]` 3d-printing the case was straightforward. Buying the kit was also straightforward. :smirk:

`[MIDDLE]` Soldering the first half was not so straightforward. Certain SMDs were tough to get right and I had to switch to a thinner soldering tip and a higher temperature. This took me upwards of a month of on-and-off effort. :weary:

`[RIGHT]` Verifying that all RGB components and switches worked was tricky. Lesson learned: not all short circuits are visible to the naked eye -- just trust your diagnosis and reflow the joints. It worked! :triumph:

`[BELOW]` Finishing the second half only took two days! Lots was learned from going through the process for the other half. Some pictures of the final result :star_struck:
